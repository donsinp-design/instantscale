Privacy Policy

InstantScale · Last updated 3 September 2026

InstantScale does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. There is no server, no account, and no analytics.

What the app stores

All of the following is kept only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your weigh-ins: weight, impedance, and the body-composition figures derived from them
  • The people you add: name, height, date of birth, sex
  • Your scale's Bluetooth identifier and decoded data format
  • A short rolling log of the raw Bluetooth packets your scale broadcasts, used for diagnosing decoding problems

This data is removed when you delete the app.

Apple Health

If you turn on Health syncing for a person, the app writes their weight, BMI, body fat percentage and lean body mass to Apple Health. It reads your height, date of birth, biological sex, and — for the dashboard — sleep, steps, active energy, exercise minutes, resting heart rate and heart rate variability.

Health data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account, under Apple's control. InstantScale never transmits it anywhere. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth solely to listen for broadcasts from your own scale. It does not scan for, track, or record other devices outside of the setup screen, where you choose your scale from what is nearby. No location data is collected or inferred.

What we never do

  • No data leaves your device
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud storage
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, for any purpose

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Weigh-ins recorded for family members, including children, are stored only on your device and are never transmitted.

Not a medical device

InstantScale is a general fitness and wellness app. Body-composition figures are estimates produced by published population equations, not clinical measurements, and body fat, muscle, bone and water values vary with hydration. Nothing in the app diagnoses, treats, or prevents any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about health decisions.
